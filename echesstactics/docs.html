Documentation

eChessTactics

This guide explains every part of the app. It follows the in-app help, with more detail. The app is available in English and German; the flag icon at the top right switches the language, and your choice is remembered.

Getting started

  1. Open the app. A rating puzzle is waiting; the board is shown from the side of the player to move.
  2. Tap the puzzle-piece icon to choose what to train (see Puzzle modes).
  3. Press Play and make the moves on the screen. Or connect an electronic board (Connecting a board) and play on the real pieces: the LEDs show how to set up each position.
  4. Tap the magnifier to get a Stockfish analysis when the app allows it (Analysis).
Requirements: Android 9 or newer. The chess engine and the position recognition run on your phone; no internet connection and no account are needed.

The top row is a blue toolbar with these icons, from the left:

Below the toolbar is the navigation bar: < previous, the task number (tap it, type a number and confirm to jump), > next, and a Play key that moves on automatically. While it runs it becomes a stop square; a long press sets the seconds between steps. The next-key turns green once the task is solved. At the right end sits a red square that stops a running analysis. In the endgames there is also a restart key.

Under the board: Play (solve on screen), the player-to-move pawn, flip, Send FEN (send the position to a connected board), and the hint and solution keys. Further down: your rating and the current mode.

Puzzle modes

Across all modes you can solve more than 730,000 different puzzles and positions. The puzzle-piece icon lists the modes:

  • Puzzle (Rating training): a random puzzle that matches your rating, from a database of more than 400,000 puzzles.
  • Mate training: 7,200 mate puzzles in a fixed order that gets harder. It starts with 200 mate-in-1 puzzles in ten levels from easy to hard, then continues with mate in 2, 3, 4 and 5. Your progress is saved.
  • Puzzle (Themes and rating): choose tactical themes and a rating range and build your own training. It does not change your rating.
  • Endgames Mix: nearly 1,000 verified endgame positions, mixed from easy to hard.
  • Endgames Themes: the same positions by theme; the pawn endgames come first.
  • Opening tactics: more than 320,000 positions that grow out of 19 individual openings.
  • User positions: your own saved positions (shown once you have saved one).
  • PGN Viewer: open and replay PGN games (see PGN viewer).

In the endgames you play against the engine for a win or a draw, and hint and solution are always available.

Solving and rating

In the rating training your rating changes with every puzzle: +15 if you solve it on the first try, +5 on the second, −5 on the third and −15 from the fourth try on. Using the hint costs 5 points and showing the solution 10 points.

The hint and solution keys unlock after three wrong attempts. Reset rating in the menu sets your rating back to 500.

Analysis

The magnifier starts a live Stockfish analysis under the board. You decide how many variations are shown with the − / + keys in front of the first line (1 to 5; the choice is remembered). Each line starts with its score in bold, always from the point of view of the side at the bottom of the board; +0.7 means that side is slightly better, #3 a mate in three. If a forced mate for the side to move exists, only that line is shown.

Arrows on the board show the first move of each variation:

dark green: the best move   light green: other candidates   red: a losing move (it gets mated or is about two pawns worse).

When is the analysis available?

  • In tasks with a prescribed solution (rating training, mate training, themes, openings) only after three wrong attempts, and it closes again with every new task. Think of it as a special hint.
  • Always in the endgames, in the PGN viewer, in the free analysis and for every imported position (FEN or photo).

Free analysis

Open the menu and choose Free analysis. The entry is greyed out while no board is connected. It ends the current training; the screen then always shows the position on your board.

  • Place, remove and move pieces as you like. About 1.5 seconds after the last change a new analysis starts.
  • Who is to move? The colour of the piece you placed last decides: placed a white piece last, Black is to move, and vice versa. Only placing counts, lifting does not. The initial position always means White to move.
  • If a king is missing or the position is impossible, the analysis pauses and shows a hint. A position that is checkmate or stalemate is reported as such.
  • Castling: a tick box appears only while the king and rook are still on their home squares. The default is no castling; you do not have to tap anything.
  • On boards with colour LEDs the candidate moves light up too (see below).
  • Close ends the free analysis and returns to your training.

Scan a position

  1. Tap the camera icon and take a photo of a diagram, or pick an image from your files.
  2. The position is recognised on your phone. Tap any wrongly recognised piece to correct it.
  3. Set whose move it is and the castling rights with the tick boxes.
  4. Tap Release image (no more corrections afterwards). The engine works out the solution in about five seconds; a progress window shows that it is working. If a board is connected it shows the target position with its LEDs, otherwise press Play and solve on the screen.

The buttons under the board: Share sends the position as FEN text, Save stores it in your collection of user positions.

User positions

Positions you save after scanning are kept in a file on your phone. A position that is already saved is not stored twice. As soon as one is saved, User positions appears in the puzzle menu. It opens in the same screen as the endgames: you play against the engine, hint and solution are always available and the analysis can be used. The positions come in the order you saved them.

The menu entry Share own database sends the whole file through Android’s share sheet (mail, messages, cloud). It is greyed out while nothing is saved.

PGN viewer

Choose PGN Viewer in the puzzle menu. The first time you pick a PGN file (for example from the Download folder). After that the last used file opens automatically; if it is gone, the file dialog appears again. The first game opens at once.

  • The navigation bar has: previous, the move count in the middle, next, play/stop, a list key to choose another game and a folder key to open another file. Games are named after their tags (White – Black, or the event); unnamed games are called “Game 1”, “Game 2”, and so on.
  • The notation under the board is tappable. The last move is highlighted on the board.
  • Comments appear as a card over the board and disappear with the next move. Where a position has variations, the card lets you pick one; the main line is the default. To see a different one, step back and arrive at that position again. The author’s own arrows in the file are ignored.
  • Play plays one move every n seconds on the screen (long press sets n).
  • The analysis shows one variation by default and works on the position that really stands on a connected board.

With a board

  • The board navigates: play the shown move (or the move of a variation) on the board and the game goes forward; take the move back and it goes back. Pop-ups close when you move on.
  • When the pieces differ from the shown position, the LEDs show the differing squares so you can set it up.
  • Colour-LED boards (iChessOne) show the game’s next move in blue and the engine’s best move in green. If both are the same, it stays blue. All other boards show only the game’s next move.
  • A Chessnut Move is navigated with the keys in the app; its robot moves the pieces.

On sensor boards the LEDs show which squares still differ from the target position; once it is reached you solve the task directly on the board. Wrong moves are reported and the pieces have to be put back.

  • Support (Discord) – opens the electricchess Discord server for questions, bugs and wishes.
  • Reset rating – back to 500.
  • Reset mate puzzles – the mate training starts again at puzzle 1.
  • Chessnut Move player side – only active while a Chessnut Move is connected.

FAQ

Why is the magnifier greyed out?

In tasks with a prescribed solution the analysis unlocks after three wrong attempts, so it does not give the answer away. In endgames, the PGN viewer, the free analysis and imported positions it is always available.

“Free analysis” cannot be selected.

It needs a connected board. Connect one with the Bluetooth icon first.

The board does not show up.

Switch the board on, make sure Bluetooth is on and that you picked the right board type in the connection list. Allow the “Nearby devices” permission when Android asks. If it still fails, save the diagnostic log from the menu and send it on the Discord server.

Where are my positions and my rating stored?

Only on your phone. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app deletes them. Use “Share own database” to keep a copy of your positions.

Does the app use the internet?

No. The app makes no connections itself; the network permission comes from Google’s billing library for the optional tip. See the privacy policy.

Where do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

On the Discord server electricchess.

eChessTactics

Train chess tactics on your phone or on your electronic chessboard, and analyse any position with Stockfish. Connect a board and solve puzzles on the real pieces: its LEDs show you how to set up each position. No board? Play everything on screen.

More than 730,000 puzzles and positions to solve: rating training, opening tactics, mate training and endgames.

What it does

Features

Everything runs on your phone: the engine and the position recognition work offline.

Beat the Bot

Play against 70 bots with a face and a name. Every strength from 700 to 2000 has five opponents with their own style: fearful, defensive, normal, eager to attack and aggressive; the little devil plays at full strength. No helps. A green check shows that you have beaten a bot with White and with Black. A mood bar and, on boards with colour LEDs, the LED colour show how the game stands. The bots run on the open-source chess engine Carballo (GPLv3): one small settings file per bot sets its strength and style.

Puzzles and mate training

Rating training with more than 400,000 puzzles that match your rating, themes and rating ranges to pick from, more than 320,000 opening-tactics positions, and a mate training with 7,200 mate puzzles in one steady climb: mate in 1 in ten levels from easy to hard, then mate in 2, 3, 4 and 5.

Endgames against the engine

Nearly 1,000 verified endgame positions, mixed from easy to hard or by theme, with the pawn endgames first. You play against the engine for a win or a draw. Hint and solution are always available.

Endgame school

326 checked tasks in two lists, from easy to hard: win the position or hold the draw. The tablebase proves your moves in small positions; a wrong move is taken back with a short verdict. Just exercises, no pop-ups.

Play against Tal

A real game against the aggressive Patricia engine that adapts to you: it helps with sensible moves when you are worse, keeps you close when you are better and takes every tactic it sees. A smiley and, on boards with colour LEDs, the LED colour show Tal's mood. Games are saved in one PGN file; analyse them with Patricia or Stockfish.

Scan a position

Photograph a diagram from a book or magazine. The position is recognised on your device, you correct any mistakes, and the engine works out the solution. Save positions to your own collection (“User positions”), play them later, or share the whole collection.

Analysis with arrows

Live Stockfish analysis with one to five variations of your choice. The score is always shown from the point of view of the side at the bottom of the board. Arrows mark the best move (dark), other candidates (lighter) and losing moves (red).

Free analysis on your board

With a connected e-board, move the pieces however you like: the screen follows the board and a new analysis starts a moment after the last move. The piece you placed last decides who is to move. On boards with colour LEDs the candidate moves light up as well.

PGN viewer

Open any PGN file, pick a game, step through the moves, read the comments and follow the variations. With a connected board you navigate the game by playing the moves on it. The analysis follows the pieces that really stand on the board. Share the open file with one tap.

E-board PGN download

Fetch the games saved on your Chessnut (Bluetooth), Supreme T2 (USB) or eChessGateway and open them straight in the PGN viewer. On the gateway you decide whether the games are kept or deleted.

Piece sets

Choose between three piece sets: Standard, Merida and MPChess. Hold the white or black pawn below the board for about 1.5 seconds, tap a set, and it stays active on every board of the app.

PGN on colour-LED boards

Replaying a game on a Tabutronic Spectrum or an iChessOne: blue shows the move that stands in the PGN, side variations light up yellow, so you can decide at the board without looking at the display.

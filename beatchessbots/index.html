Beat Chessbots

A chess app that does one thing: you play against a chess engine that thinks for itself. On the screen of your phone or on an electronic chessboard, and always against a real opponent, never a puzzle.

Play the adaptive game in which the computer adjusts to you, or choose one of 215 bots with a face and a name: 43 strength levels from 700 to 2800, each with five playing styles. Beat a bot with White and with Black to earn its green check.

What it does

Features

The engine runs on your phone, so everything works offline.

Beat the Bot

Pick a strength group, then one of the 43 levels from 700 to 2800, then one of its five opponents. Every bot has its own picture and name. The five styles are Water (fearful), Air (careful), Normal, Fire (eager to attack) and Lightning (aggressive). A green check shows that you beat a bot with White and with Black.

The rematch

You beat a bot with one colour only? Right after the mate, the robot appears on the screen and demands a rematch with the other colour. Accept it with one tap, or cancel.

Adaptive game

A real game against the computer that adapts to you: it keeps the game close, helps with sensible moves when it is clearly ahead and takes every tactic it sees. The queen in the bar below the board shows how the game stands.

The queen shows the mood

A bar below the board shows who is ahead. The queen slides along it and changes with the position, from radiant to caught in a storm and finally struck by lightning and fire. On boards with colour LEDs the LEDs follow the same colours.

Analysis with arrows

Live Stockfish analysis with one to five variations. The score is always shown from the point of view of the side at the bottom of the board. Arrows mark the best move, other candidates and losing moves. Taking a move back or using the analysis during a bot game is possible, but that game then does not count for the green check.

Free analysis on your board

With a connected e-board, move the pieces however you like: the screen follows the board and a new analysis starts a moment after the last move.

Games and positions

Every game can be shared as a PGN file or opened in the PGN analysis. Enter or share any position as FEN and set it up on your board.

Piece sets

Four piece sets: Standard, Merida, MPChess and Bryan’s Italian, which is the default. Choose one in the menu; it stays active on every board of the app.

Nine languages

German, English, Spanish, Italian, French, Polish, Hindi, Turkish and Arabic. The moves are written with English piece letters in every language except German.

The queen

Six moods

From radiant when you are clearly ahead to lightning and fire when the game is lost.

Left to right: you are well ahead, a little ahead, balanced, a little behind, clearly behind, lost. The score comes from the engine and is always seen from the player’s side.

Look and feel

Screenshots

Dark sheets, big pictures, built around the board.

Hardware

Supported boards

Set up the start position on your e-board, and then play your moves on the real pieces. The robot’s answer is shown on the screen and on the board’s LEDs.

Good to know

Details

  • No ads and no account. The engines run on your device.
  • Android 9 or newer. Portrait and landscape.
  • Questions, bugs, wishes: the Discord server electricchess ↗.
  • Engines: the games are played by the open-source engine Virgin (MIT licence); the analysis uses Stockfish (GPLv3).
  • Piece sets: Merida (GPLv2+, Armando Hernandez Marroquin), MPChess (GPLv3+, Maxime Chupin) and Bryan’s Italian (a gift of Bryan Withby).
  • Also from this site: eChessTactics, puzzles and training for the screen and for e-boards.

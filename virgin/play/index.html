Virginia is the adaptive opponent of the Virgin engine. She keeps the game close and winnable for you; the engine runs on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. When a pawn reaches the last rank you choose the new piece. Draws follow the usual rules (stalemate, three-fold repetition, fifty moves, too little material). The queen shows how the game stands from your point of view, from radiant to lightning and fire. More bots, five playing styles, analysis and electronic boards: Beat Chessbots. Pieces: Bryan’s Italian, a gift of Bryan Withby. Board logic: chess.js (BSD). Engine: Virgin 0.8.2 (MIT).

eChessTactics

01

Android chess trainer for the screen and for electronic chessboards: more than 730,000 puzzles and positions to solve (rating training, opening tactics, 7,200 mate puzzles, endgames against the engine), a real game against the adaptive engine Tal, and 71 chess bots with their own styles to play against. Stockfish analysis with arrows, free analysis on your board, diagram scanning and a PGN viewer. Currently in closed testing on Google Play.

eChessGw

02

Open-source BLE gateway for chess computer modules with a ChessLink/Mode-B interface. An ESP32-C3 wirelessly connects cable-based chess computers to supported BLE e-boards and translates their protocols at runtime -- including a standalone mode with no cable computer required, and automatic PGN game recording with USB retrieval.

ESP32-C3BLEChessLinkPGNPlatformIO

03

Bluetooth LE gateway for the Manya Cynus chess robot. An ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C3 translates between the Cynus protocol and ChessLink-compatible chess software.

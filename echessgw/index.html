eChessGw

A small wireless gateway for electronic chessboards. It connects a cable-based chess computer (MILLENNIUM King, Mephisto Phoenix) to any of several wireless e-boards over BLE: one firmware, automatic board detection at runtime.

With no cable computer present, the gateway can also act as a ChessLink or Chessnut board itself, so wireless chess software such as BearChess, PicoChess or Chess PGN Master can connect directly.

Flash it in your browser ↗ GitHub ↗

eChessGw is a DIY project: all information, the source and the wiring are on GitHub. Questions? Come to the Discord server electricchess.

What it does

Features

One firmware for the ESP32-C3 SuperMini. The gateway works out at runtime what is connected.

Cable computer to wireless board

A chess computer with a ChessLink / Mode-B cable interface talks to a wireless e-board through the gateway. The gateway translates the protocols at runtime, including castling, en passant and promotion on the Manya Cynus.

Standalone mode

No cable computer needed: the gateway advertises itself as a ChessLink or Chessnut board over BLE for wireless chess software (PicoChess, BearChess, Chess PGN Master).

Automatic PGN recording

Games are recorded automatically, including checkmate detection and a manual result-signal gesture. Fetch them over USB with a simple physical gesture and a small Windows tool, or over BLE with Chessnut emulation.

Engine against engine

Two engines can play each other from a virtual board inside the gateway, for example BearChess against a Mephisto Phoenix, with the moves shown and carried out on a real board.

Follow an e-book game

The Manya Cynus protocol can be presented as a Chessnut board, so the vision technology of an app can follow a game from an e-book on a real board.

Flash from the browser

No toolchain needed: connect the ESP32-C3 SuperMini by USB, open the installer in Chrome or Edge and press Connect. Source code and the PGN retrieval tool are on GitHub.

Supported boards

Cable side: MILLENNIUM King and Mephisto Phoenix. Wireless side: the boards below.

MILLENNIUM Supreme T2 BTChessnut Air / GO / ProCertaboManya Cynus

Certabo pieces are read in at every start: 32 pieces in the starting position plus a spare white queen on d3 and a spare black queen on d6. The four centre squares blink three times when done; then remove the two queens. The gateway is an independent project.
